(http://mocap.cs.cmu.edu), created with funding from NSF EIA-0196217, via the
BVH conversion at github.com/una-dinosauria/cmu-mocap. Trials used: 76_01,
13_17, 111_19 (attacks); 140_01, 90_18, 113_08 (deaths); 111_03 (fall, crawl);
133_01, 133_02 (crawls); 140_03, 111_21, 140_04 (lying on the ground).
